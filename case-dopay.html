Case study · Dopay

Payroll, paid instantly.

Payroll and digital banking for the cash-paid workforce in Egypt and MENA - employers upload salary files, workers get a Mastercard card, a wallet, and often their first real account.

2Products: worker app + employer payroll
4Core partners: Bank ABC, AFS, Masria, Mastercard
ISO 8583Transaction standard, PCI-DSS aligned

01

The problem

  • Millions of workers in Egypt are paid in cash, with no salary visibility and no bank account.
  • Employers needed a reliable way to upload, approve, and settle payroll at scale - including refunds and reversals.
  • Identity verification failures were blocking workers from getting onto the product at all.

02

What I built

  • Dopay app (iOS / Android) - salary visibility, instant withdrawal, and the full card lifecycle.
  • Dopay for Business (web + mobile) - payroll uploads, approvals, settlements, refunds, and reversals, backed by real-time payroll APIs and validated ledger entries.
  • OCR-based KYC with backend validation to cut failed verifications.
  • Operational dashboards tracking payroll activation, success rates, and payout turnaround.

03

Trade-offs

  • Speed vs. network compliance. Card features had to meet Mastercard network rules, PCI-DSS, and ISO 8583 from day one - scope was shaped around what could ship compliantly.
  • Launch vs. resilience. Bulk payments and edge cases were simulated before scale-up, because a failed payroll run hits every worker at once.

04

The result

  • Worker and employer products live with Bank ABC, AFS, Masria Digital Payments, and Mastercard.
  • Fewer failed identity verifications after the OCR + backend validation change.
  • Payroll operations measured end to end - activation, success rate, and payout turnaround - on shared dashboards.
